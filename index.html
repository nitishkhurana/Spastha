◆ Beyond the demo

The part that actually moves the needle

Explaining a document is table stakes — every legal-AI demo does that. The features below are what turn "I understand my lease now" into "I got a better lease."

01

Negotiation script, not just a summary

Most people who understand a bad clause still don't know how to push back. Spashta drafts the actual message — polite, specific, citing the clause — so the hardest part is just hitting send.

02

"Prep me for a lawyer" mode

A one-page briefing — facts, red flags, exact questions to ask — cuts a ₹2,000–5,000 first consultation down to 15 focused minutes instead of 45 spent explaining the basics.

03

WhatsApp, not a website

The people who most need this rarely start at a URL. A WhatsApp number you forward a photo of a document to meets them where they already are — free tier covers a real pilot.

⚠Not a lawyer. Spashta explains documents and drafts communications for you — it doesn't replace a licensed advocate, and nothing here is legal advice for your specific situation. For anything with real money or real risk attached, use the "Prep for a lawyer" output to get a proper consultation, faster.
